Chess Quick Guide

1. The Goal of the Game
Simple: Checkmate the opponent’s king.
Checkmate = the king is under attack and cannot escape.
♟️ 2. How the Pieces Move
  • King — Moves one square in any direction.
  • Queen — Any number of squares in any direction.
  • Rook — Straight (horizontal/vertical), any number.
  • Bishop — Diagonally only.
  • Knight — L-shape (2+1), can jump over pieces.
  • Pawn — Moves forward. First move can be 1 or 2 squares. Captures diagonally.
♟️ 3. Special Moves
  • Castling — King moves 2 squares toward rook; rook jumps next to king. Conditions: neither moved, no pieces between, king not in/through check.
  • En Passant — If an opponent pawn moves 2 squares and lands beside yours, you may capture as if it moved 1 — only immediately on the next move.
  • Pawn Promotion — Pawn that reaches the far rank becomes a queen (usually) or rook/bishop/knight.
♟️ 4. Check, Checkmate & Stalemate
  • Check — King is attacked. You must move king, block, or capture the attacker.
  • Checkmate — King is in check with no escape → game over.
  • Stalemate — Not in check but no legal move → draw.
♟️ 5. Basic Playing Rules
  • White moves first.
  • You must move if you can.
  • You cannot put your own king in check.
  • Touch-move (tournaments): if you touch a piece you must move it.
